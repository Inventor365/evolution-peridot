# POCO F6 / Redmi Turbo 3 (peridot)

### September 2026 Release

## Highlights

* **Yuki Charging Service v5** — major charging subsystem overhaul with full 120W support, improved charging stability, removal of unwanted charging caps, and fixes for mid-charge current degradation.
* **Thermal Engine Overhaul** — significantly reworked charging, gaming, high-performance, and daily-use thermal behavior, including improved regional parity.
* **Adaptive AUTO Power Profiles** — smarter workload detection with dedicated handling for gaming, heavy compute, everyday use, and screen-off idle.
* **Native Floating Quick Settings Panels** — redesigned Power Profile and Charging Control selectors with a cleaner Material 3 experience.
* **XiaomiParts UI Overhaul** — refreshed Power Profile and Charging Control interfaces with live telemetry and Pixel/Material You styling.
* **WALT & PowerHAL Refinements** — improved CPU scheduling behavior, burst responsiveness, power efficiency, and system stability.
* **Telephony & Security Fixes** — VoLTE/VoWiFi caller ID improvements and updated Play Integrity / TrickyStore handling.

---

## Yuki Charging & 120W Fast Charging

This release brings a substantial overhaul to the charging subsystem.

* Reworked charging state handling for **near-zero background activity while unplugged**, improving idle efficiency.
* Improved cable insertion and USB-PD/PPS negotiation handling to prevent charging state races and inconsistent activation.
* Improved plug/unplug reliability and protection against transient charging-state changes.
* **Full 120W charging capability unlocked**, removing previous software-imposed current restrictions.
* Removed unwanted **80% / 85% charging limitations** across charging modes, allowing charging to reach 100% normally.
* Fixed the previously observed **mid-charge current degradation**, including the significant current drop occurring during the lower-to-mid battery range.
* Improved interaction between the charging system and thermal management so charging current remains consistent instead of being unnecessarily reclaimed by competing controls.
* Strengthened temperature protection and ensured charging-related overrides are properly released when conditions require it.
* Added improved charging diagnostics and telemetry for easier validation of charging behavior.

**Result:** faster, more consistent charging with proper 120W capability, fewer charging-state glitches, and significantly cleaner behavior when the device is unplugged.

---

## Thermal Engine Overhaul

The thermal configuration has been substantially reworked across charging, gaming, performance, and everyday workloads.

### Charging

* Improved charging thermal behavior using more appropriate battery-temperature handling.
* Raised thermal operating limits to better match the device's actual charging capabilities.
* Increased the available thermal ceiling for high-current charging while retaining hard safety limits.
* Improved coordination between charging and thermal management to prevent unnecessary current reductions.

### Gaming & High Performance

Gaming profiles have received one of the largest tuning passes in this release.

* Reduced overly aggressive stock throttling across gaming and high-performance modes.
* Removed premature GPU restrictions that could force the GPU down to very low frequencies under relatively low temperatures.
* Introduced a more gradual GPU thermal response instead of abrupt frequency drops.
* Raised CPU thermal floors so sustained workloads retain significantly more performance before throttling.
* Improved Prime, Gold, and Silver cluster behavior under sustained load.
* Reworked battery thermal handling in gaming workloads for more predictable performance.
* Updated high-performance and esports behavior to better reflect the actual thermal capabilities of the hardware.
* Brought regional performance profiles in line with their global counterparts to eliminate unnecessary regional throttling differences.

### Daily Usage

* Removed unnecessary GPU restrictions from everyday-use profiles.
* Improved Normal, Video, Navigation, and similar profiles to provide better responsiveness without sacrificing power efficiency.
* Added a dedicated lightweight UI profile for common workloads such as messaging, social media, browsing, and general app usage.

**Result:** less unnecessary throttling, smoother sustained gaming performance, better GPU responsiveness, and more consistent behavior across workloads.

---

## Adaptive AUTO Power Profile

AUTO has been rebuilt into a much more intelligent workload-aware profile.

Instead of treating AUTO as a simple middle ground, it now dynamically adapts the system according to what the device is actually doing.

* Added automatic workload classification for everyday use, gaming, and sustained compute workloads.
* Added dedicated **Heavy Compute** handling for workloads such as development, compilation, emulation, rendering, and other sustained CPU-heavy tasks.
* Expanded application awareness across a broad range of games and demanding applications.
* Improved GPU workload detection to prevent rapid switching caused by short-lived load spikes.
* Added a dedicated low-power screen-off idle state to significantly reduce unnecessary background power consumption.
* Improved transitions between active and idle states to prevent profile oscillation.
* Charging-aware profile handling prevents AUTO from fighting the active charging configuration.
* Improved service reliability so AUTO continues managing the device correctly even under Android background process management.
* Improved profile state synchronization and restoration across reboots, charging sessions, and manual profile changes.

### Power Profile Stability

* Improved ownership and synchronization of profile state across SystemUI and XiaomiParts.
* Fixed cases where the active profile could become unknown after charging completed.
* Improved switching between AUTO and manually selected profiles.
* Prevented stale background profile decisions from overwriting newly selected user profiles.

**Result:** AUTO is now a genuine adaptive profile rather than simply a balanced preset — using more power when the workload actually needs it and backing off aggressively during light or idle usage.

## Power Profile & Charging Control UI

Quick Settings controls have been redesigned around native floating panels.

### Power Profiles

* Added a native floating Power Profile selector directly from Quick Settings.
* Supports **Balanced, Battery Saver, Performance, Gaming, and Auto**.
* Added live selection indicators and cleaner profile presentation.
* Improved transitions and interaction while keeping the Quick Settings shade available underneath.

### Charging Control

* Added a native floating Charging Control selector.
* Quickly switch between **Slow, Fast, and Superfast** charging modes.
* Added live status indication and dedicated mode presentation.

### HTSR

* Integrated High Touch Sampling Rate controls directly into the Power Profile experience.
* Gaming and Performance modes can automatically enable HTSR.
* Balanced, Battery Saver, and Auto baseline modes restore the normal touch configuration.
* AUTO can dynamically increase touch sampling behavior when active gaming is detected.
* Fixed the reliability issue where HTSR could revert shortly after being enabled.

## WALT Scheduler & Performance

The CPU scheduler and performance stack received another round of refinement.

* Enforced consistent **WALT governor behavior across all CPU clusters**.
* Tuned scheduler migration behavior to allow demanding workloads to reach the Prime cluster more naturally.
* Improved Prime-core scheduling behavior under sustained workloads.
* Corrected performance-limit handling under WALT.
* Reverted scheduler changes that were found to increase FPS variance and hurt low-percentile frame times.
* Improved the balance between responsiveness and energy efficiency rather than simply forcing higher frequencies.

### PowerHAL

* Re-enabled CPU low-power behavior during interactive usage.
* Tuned interaction boost duration and frequency floors for a better responsiveness/efficiency balance.
* Improved CPU ramp-down behavior between short interactions.
* Fixed a PowerHAL stability issue that could result in service crashes.
* Added additional validation around performance configuration to prevent invalid tuning from causing PowerHAL failures.
* Updated the default boot thermal state to a balanced Normal profile.

**Result:** better burst responsiveness without permanently keeping high-performance cores active, smoother scheduler behavior, and improved sustained performance consistency.

# Overall

This release is primarily focused on **power management, charging, thermals, and sustained performance**.

The biggest changes are not simply higher frequency limits or more aggressive performance settings. The underlying behavior has been reworked so the device can:

* **Charge faster and more consistently**
* **Actually utilize its 120W charging capability**
* **Avoid unnecessary charging and thermal restrictions**
* **Reduce screen-off idle drain**
* **Adapt performance automatically to the workload**
* **Sustain higher gaming performance before throttling**
* **Maintain better responsiveness during everyday use**
* **Scale back aggressively when the workload doesn't require performance**
* **Keep CPU scheduling and power management more consistent**
* **Provide a cleaner and more cohesive user experience**

This release is essentially a pass focused on making the hardware behave closer to what it is actually capable of, while keeping efficiency and thermal safety intact.
